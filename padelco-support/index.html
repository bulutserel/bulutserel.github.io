FAQ

How do I play?

Move with the joystick on the left side of the screen. Hold the racket button to charge your shot and release it to hit. Hold the sprint button to run, but keep an eye on your stamina.

How does scoring work?

Real padel scoring: 15, 30, 40, game. The first team to win 3 games wins the match.

Can I use the glass walls?

Yes! The ball may bounce off the glass after it lands. Hitting the ball into your own back glass sends it over the net. Fences are different: if the ball touches a fence before it bounces, you lose the point.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Multiplayer → Create a room, then share the 4-letter room code. Your friends enter the code and tap Join with code. Online play needs an internet connection.

"Update required" message

Your version of the game is older than the server. Please update Padelco from the App Store.

How do I change the language or turn off music?

Use the TR / EN buttons and the music note button at the top right of the main menu.
