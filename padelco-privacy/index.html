Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Padelco is developed by Bulut Serel. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect personal data.

No accounts, no tracking

  • Padelco does not require an account, name, email or login.
  • The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

Settings on your device

Your language and music preferences are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Online play

When you play online, your device connects to our game server to create or join a room. During the match, the server only receives game data such as player movement, shots and the selected character. To prevent abuse, the server briefly keeps your IP address in memory to limit how many rooms can be created per minute. This is not stored or linked to you. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical connection logs for operating and protecting the service.

Room codes are random and only valid while the room is open. When all players leave, the room is closed and its data is deleted.

Children

Padelco does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
